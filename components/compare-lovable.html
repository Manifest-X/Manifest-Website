Manifest vs Lovable

Lovable meters your building with credits. Manifest runs inside the Claude you already use: building is conversation, publishing is flat.

Choose Lovable if…

  • You want one web app that builds and hosts in a single place.
  • You like remixing community projects as starting points.
  • You don't use Claude.

Choose Manifest if…

  • You already work in Claude or Claude Code and want building covered by that subscription.
  • You want flat per-project pricing with no credit meter.
  • You want an open-source framework underneath and your code exportable from day one.
  • You want staging, rollback, and free teammates invited by email.

Common questions

Can I bring a Lovable project?

Front ends that build to static files publish as-is. Backends stay where they already run; your site keeps calling them.

Do I need Claude?

Yes, and that's the design: one subscription builds everything, with no second builder to pay for.

Does Manifest charge per AI edit?

No. Building happens in your Claude chat. Manifest charges a flat rate per project, and free is free.
