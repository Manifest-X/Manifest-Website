Manifest vs Lovable

Lovable consumes monthly credits to build projects. Manifest is free to use in your Claude account.

Choose Lovable if…

  • You want one web app that builds and hosts in a single place.
  • You like remixing community projects as starting points.
  • You want one account fee covering many small projects.
  • You don't use Claude.

Choose Manifest if…

  • You already work in Claude or Claude Code and want building covered by that subscription.
  • You want flat per-project pricing with no credit meter.
  • You want the code, content, and backend to be yours, exportable from day one.
  • You want staging, rollback, and free teammates invited by email.

Common questions

Can I bring a Lovable project?

Yes. Give Claude your Lovable site's address and Manifest rebuilds it as a carbon copy, yours to edit and host from then on.

Do I need Claude?

Yes, and that's the design: one subscription builds everything, with no second builder to pay for.

Does Manifest charge per AI edit?

No. Building happens in your Claude chats.
