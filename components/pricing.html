Common questions

Something else? Email the team.

What counts as a project?

One site or app with its own URL and deployments. Plans apply per project.

What happens if I cancel?

Your site stays live. Pro runs to the end of the billing period you've paid for, then the project returns to Free: the badge comes back and Pro features lock. Nothing is deleted.

Do teammates cost extra?

No. Pricing is per project, never per person.

Can I use my own domain on Free?

Yes, with automatic certificates.

What is the badge?

A small "Made with Manifest" link on Free sites. Pro removes it.

Will my site appear in the showcase?

Free projects may be featured in the curated showcase. Pro projects are never listed.

How is billing handled?

Per project through Stripe, monthly or yearly. Upgrade from the dashboard, or just ask Claude.
