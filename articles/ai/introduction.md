# Introduction

Build and publish websites, apps, and games just by asking your AI app.

---

## What is Manifest?

Manifest puts your projects on the internet. Whether you've made a website, a web app, a game, or a tool for your team with an AI app like Claude, or want to, Manifest is where it lives: it gives your project a web address, keeps it running, and lets you change it any time by asking your AI.

You don't need to write code, rent a server, or learn any technical tools. You talk to your AI app, and it uses Manifest to do the rest.

---

## How it works

1. **Add Manifest to your AI app.** You do this once. Manifest joins your AI app as a *connector* or *plugin*, an add-on that lets the AI use Manifest for you.
2. **Sign in.** The first time, a page opens in your browser so you can sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email address. This creates your free Manifest account.
3. **Ask for what you want.** Your AI builds the project and publishes it with Manifest.
4. **Share your link.** Manifest gives your project an address like `my-bakery.manifestx.ai` that anyone can visit. You can switch to your own address, like `mybakery.com`, whenever you like.

> `Make me a website for my bakery and put it online`{copy}
> `Build a memory card game I can share with my class`{copy}
> `Make a booking page for my yoga classes`{copy}

After that, changes work the same way. Ask your AI to update the project, check the result, then make it live. The [Quickstart](/docs/ai/quickstart) walks through it step by step.

---

## What you can do

- **Publish** a project and get a web address for it.
- **Preview changes** at a separate address before anyone else sees them.
- **Undo** a change by going back to an earlier version.
- **Use your own domain**, with the security certificate handled for you.
- **Invite people** to work on your project from their own AI app.
- **Back up your project** to GitHub, and share it with the people you work with.
- **See your visitors**: how many came, which pages they read, and where they came from.
- **Keep track of your projects** in the Manifest [dashboard](/docs/ai/projects/manage).

::: brand icon="lucide:info"
Manifest hosts projects that run in the visitor's browser: websites, web apps, games, dashboards, and more. It doesn't run programs on a server. If your project uses a separate service for accounts or stored data, that service keeps working and your project connects to it.
:::

## Words you'll see

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| AI app | An app you chat with, like Claude. |
| Connector | An add-on that lets your AI app use another service, here Manifest. |
| Plugin | A bundle that adds the connector plus extra know-how and shortcuts to your AI app. |
| Project | One thing you've made: a website, app, game, or anything else that runs in a browser. |
| Publish | Put your project on the internet. |
| Staging | A preview copy of your project at its own address, for checking changes before anyone else sees them. |
| Live | The version of your project everyone sees. Also called *production*. |
| Domain | A web address you own, like `example.com`. |
| Dashboard | The Manifest website where you see all your projects, at `dash.manifestx.dev`. |

## Building with code?

Manifest also has an open-source framework for building websites and apps in HTML. Your AI uses it when it builds a project from scratch, and Manifest hosting works with projects made other ways too, such as React or plain HTML. If you write code yourself, see the [Framework docs](/docs/framework).
