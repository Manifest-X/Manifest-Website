# Introduction

Put a website online just by asking your AI app.

---

## What is Manifest?

Manifest puts websites and apps on the internet. If you've made a website with an AI app like Claude, or want to, Manifest is where it lives: it gives your site a web address, keeps it running, and lets you change it any time by asking your AI.

You don't need to write code, rent a server, or learn any technical tools. You talk to your AI app, and it uses Manifest to do the rest.

---

## How it works

1. **Add Manifest to your AI app.** You do this once. Manifest joins your AI app as a *connector* or *plugin*, an add-on that lets the AI use Manifest for you.
2. **Sign in.** The first time, a page opens in your browser so you can sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email address. This creates your free Manifest account.
3. **Ask for what you want.** For example: `Make me a website for my bakery and put it online`{.prompt copy}. Your AI builds the site and publishes it with Manifest.
4. **Share your link.** Manifest gives your site an address like `my-bakery.manifestx.ai` that anyone can visit. You can switch to your own address, like `mybakery.com`, whenever you like.

After that, changes work the same way. Ask your AI to update the site, check the result, then make it live.

---

## What you can do

- **Publish** a site and get a web address for it.
- **Preview changes** at a separate address before your visitors see them.
- **Undo** a change by going back to an earlier version.
- **Use your own domain**, with the security certificate handled for you.
- **Invite people** to work on your site from their own AI app.
- **Back up your project** to GitHub, and share it with the people you work with.
- **See your visitors**: how many came, which pages they read, and where they came from.
- **Keep track of your projects** in the Manifest [dashboard](/docs/ai/site/manage).

::: brand icon="lucide:info"
Manifest hosts websites and web apps that run in the visitor's browser. It doesn't run programs on a server. If your site already uses a separate service for accounts or stored data, that service keeps working and your site connects to it.
:::

## Words you'll see

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| AI app | An app you chat with, like Claude. |
| Connector | An add-on that lets your AI app use another service, here Manifest. |
| Plugin | A bundle that adds the connector plus extra know-how and shortcuts to your AI app. |
| Project | One website or app in Manifest. |
| Publish | Put your site on the internet. |
| Staging | A preview copy of your site at its own address, for checking changes before visitors see them. |
| Live | The version of your site your visitors see. Also called *production*. |
| Domain | A web address you own, like `example.com`. |
| Dashboard | The Manifest website where you see all your projects, at `dash.manifestx.dev`. |

## Building with code?

Manifest also has an open-source framework for building websites and apps in HTML. Your AI uses it when it builds a site from scratch, and Manifest hosting works with sites made other ways too, such as React or plain HTML. If you write code yourself, see the [Framework docs](/docs/framework).
