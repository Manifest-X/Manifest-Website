# Your First Site

From nothing to a live website in a few minutes, just by asking.

---

## What you need

- **Claude Code** — the version of Claude that can work with files. It's the **Code** tab of the Claude desktop app. New to it? See [what Claude Code is](/docs/ai/setup/claude#what-is-claude-code).
- **Manifest added to Claude** — install the [plugin](/docs/ai/setup/plugin) once and you're set.
- **Node.js** — a free tool Claude uses to run your site. Get it at <a href="https://nodejs.org" target="_blank">nodejs.org</a>; Claude will tell you if it's missing.

That's it. No account to create up front — you'll sign in the first time your site goes online.

---

## Step 1 — Ask for a site

Open Claude Code in a new folder and describe what you want, in your own words:

`Make me a one-page website for my pottery studio, with a photo gallery and opening hours, and show me a preview`{.prompt copy}

Claude sets up the project, builds the page, and opens a preview on your computer. Nothing is online yet — this copy is only visible to you.

Be as specific or as vague as you like. Colors, a business name, links you want, a site you admire: everything you mention shapes the result, and everything you leave out, Claude decides.

---

## Step 2 — Make it yours

Look at the preview and say what should change, as you would to a person:

`Use a warmer color palette, something like terracotta`{.prompt copy}

`Make the opening hours easier to read on a phone`{.prompt copy}

`Add a short "about me" section with a placeholder photo`{.prompt copy}

Each change shows up in the preview. Keep going until it looks right — there's no limit, and nothing is published until you say so.

---

## Step 3 — Put it online

`Put my site online`{.prompt copy}

The first time, a page opens in your browser: sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email address, and select **Approve**. That creates your free Manifest account and connects it to Claude — you won't be asked again.

A moment later Claude hands you the address where your site now lives, something like `my-pottery-studio.manifestx.ai`. Open it, send it to a friend — it's a real website, online for as long as you want it to be.

---

## Where to go next

- **Keep editing.** Ask for changes any time, then `Put my site online`{.prompt copy} again. See [Edit](/docs/ai/site/edit).
- **Check changes before visitors see them** with a staging address: [Publish](/docs/ai/site/publish#staging-then-live).
- **Use your own domain**, like `mypottery.com`: [Publish](/docs/ai/site/publish#use-your-own-domain).
- **Invite someone** to work on the site from their own Claude: [Manage](/docs/ai/site/manage).
- **See your visitors** — how many came and what they read: [Manage](/docs/ai/site/manage).
