# Manage

Your projects, plans, teams, and visitors.

---

## Dashboard

Sign in at <a href="https://dash.manifestx.dev" target="_blank">dash.manifestx.dev</a> the same way you did in Claude. Each project is a card with its address, plan, team, and status: **Live**, **Staging** (published to staging only), or **Draft** (not published yet).

When you have several projects, Claude asks which one you mean before changing anything.

> `Which Manifest projects do I have?`{copy}

---

## Plans

Each project has its own plan, Free or Pro, so you only pay for the projects that need more. Compare them in the <a href="/#pricing" target="_blank">pricing section</a>.

> `Upgrade this project to Pro`{copy}
> `Manage my billing`{copy}

Only the project's owner can upgrade. Claude replies with a secure checkout link, and the project switches within seconds. The billing link lets you change or cancel the plan, update your card, and download invoices. If you cancel, Pro runs to the end of the paid period, then the project moves to Free and stays online.

---

## Teams

On Pro, invite people to a project. Each person works from their own Claude with their own sign-in, and only sees the projects they're invited to.

> `Invite sam@example.com to this project`{copy}

- **Members** can edit and publish. **Admins** can also invite people.
- To start editing, the invitee opens a new Code session and asks Claude to `download the latest published version`{.prompt copy}.
- To remove someone, ask `remove sam@example.com`{.prompt copy}. This works on any plan.

---

## Analytics

> `How many people visited this week, and where did they come from?`{copy}

Claude reports views, visits, top pages, sources, countries, and devices: the last 7 days on Free, all time on Pro. No cookies are used and no one is identified.

To leave out your own visits, open your live address once with `?mnfst-team`{copy} at the end, in each browser you use. Add `?mnfst-team=off`{copy} to count that browser again.
